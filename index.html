Good to Know

Policy & Closing Notes

To give every client the experience they deserve, we ask that you arrive on time, come with clean and detangled hair, and communicate any concerns before your appointment begins. A deposit is required to hold your slot — this is applied toward your final balance.

Cancellations with less than 48 hours' notice may result in a forfeited deposit, as our braiders reserve their time specifically for you. We deeply appreciate your understanding and respect for their craft and schedule.

Photography of your completed style may be taken for our portfolio and social media — please let us know in advance if you'd prefer to opt out. Your privacy is important to us and we never share your personal information.
